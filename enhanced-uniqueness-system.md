# Enhanced Question Uniqueness & Complexity System

## 🚨 **Problem Addressed**
The original system was generating questions that were:
- **Too similar** to each other despite randomization
- **Too straightforward** and lacking complexity
- **Predictable** in structure and approach
- **Easy to guess** without deep expertise

## 🔥 **Advanced Multi-Layer Solution**

### **1. 🌡️ Maximum Creativity Configuration**
```typescript
// Cranked to maximum for extreme diversity
temperature: 1.0,        // Maximum randomness for creativity
topP: 0.95,             // Very broad nucleus sampling  
topK: 60,               // Consider more tokens for variety
```

### **2. 🚫 Sophisticated Pattern Detection**
**5-Layer Pattern Analysis:**
- **Opening Phrase Patterns**: First 8 words to prevent similar starts
- **Technical Concept Extraction**: Identifies overused service combinations  
- **Question Structure Patterns**: Tracks question types (which/what/how/when)
- **Options Patterns**: Analyzes answer choice structures
- **Scenario Complexity Indicators**: Monitors business context usage

**Enhanced Pattern Storage**: 100 patterns per type (vs. 50 before)

### **3. 🎯 Scenario-Based Complexity Approaches**

#### **Instead of Simple Approaches:**
❌ ~~"Match services with features"~~
❌ ~~"Order deployment steps"~~
❌ ~~"Select multiple benefits"~~

#### **Now Complex Analytical Scenarios:**
✅ **Matching**: "Match complex business scenarios to optimal architectural solutions with trade-off analysis"
✅ **Ordering**: "Sequence complex disaster recovery procedures with multiple failure scenarios and dependencies"  
✅ **Multiple Select**: "Identify all factors that must be considered when migrating legacy systems with zero downtime"
✅ **MCQ**: "Analyze complex trade-offs between competing architectural decisions under strict constraints"

### **4. 🧠 Professional-Level Question Requirements**

#### **Forbidden Question Types:**
- ❌ Simple definition questions
- ❌ Straightforward "which service" questions
- ❌ Basic concept matching
- ❌ Linear procedures without conditions
- ❌ Questions answerable by juniors

#### **Required Question Characteristics:**
- ✅ Multi-step analytical thinking required
- ✅ Conflicting requirements that need balancing  
- ✅ Hidden complexity or non-obvious considerations
- ✅ Real-world constraints (budget, time, compliance)
- ✅ Trade-off analysis and business impact understanding
- ✅ Professional judgment, not just technical knowledge

### **5. 🚨 Advanced Avoidance System**

**Categorized Pattern Avoidance:**
```
🚫 FORBIDDEN OPENING PHRASES: "given a company that", "which aws service", "when implementing a"
🚫 OVERUSED CONCEPTS: Avoid focusing primarily on: lambda, s3, ec2, rds
🚫 OVERUSED QUESTION TYPES: Avoid starting with: which, what questions  
🚫 OVERUSED SCENARIO ELEMENTS: Don't reuse: company, requirements, performance
```

**Mandatory Alternatives Enforced:**
- Completely different industries and business contexts
- Novel technical scenarios not seen before
- Diverse question structures and analytical approaches
- Fresh constraints, requirements, and edge cases

### **6. 🎲 Industry & Context Rotation**
**Dynamic Business Contexts:**
- Healthcare startups vs Enterprise manufacturing vs Financial services
- Regulated industries vs High-growth startups vs Global enterprises  
- Legacy modernization vs Greenfield development vs Compliance-heavy environments

**Varied Technical Perspectives:**
- Solutions architect vs DevOps engineer vs Security specialist
- Cost optimization vs Performance vs Compliance vs Scalability focus
